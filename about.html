The story

Small batches,
big feelings.

Gen’s Arts is a one-woman studio working in resin and wax. Personalised photo frames, name lights, jewellery, keychains and hand-poured candles — each made to order for baptisms, weddings, anniversaries and every moment worth keeping.

Every piece is set by hand, petal by petal, letter by letter. Candles are poured from pure refined wax in small batches. Nothing is mass-produced, and no two pieces are ever exactly alike.
